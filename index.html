        Questions
        
    
        Is the app free?
        Yes. Demo accounts, the Piggy bank, 38 lessons and NOVA are free, with no subscriptions or purchases inside. NOVA answers 5 questions a day, or 100 a day with a connected real account.
        How does CRYPTO NOVA make money?
        Through the exchange's partner program. If you open a BingX account through our link, the exchange pays us part of its own fee. Your fees don't change, and we take no share of your profit.
        Does the app trade for me?
        No. There is no automated trading. You see every trade of the system and decide yourself whether to repeat it on your own exchange account.
        Where is my money?
        In demo there is no real money. If you trade for real, the money stays in your own exchange account: we can't see, move or withdraw it. We never ask for passwords, SMS codes, seed phrases or exchange keys.
        Are the results real?
        The public portfolio is a demo account: virtual money at real prices, running since 18 October 2026. Tests on history are not a promise about the future. Losing trades and losing forecasts are published next to the winners.
        Is this financial advice?
        No. CRYPTO NOVA is education and demo accounts. The decisions are yours, and crypto trading is high risk.
        Who is behind CRYPTO NOVA?
        The app is operated by Dmytro Sukhyna. Write to cryptonova.app@gmail.com or to @crypto_nova_support on Telegram.
        I have an old Pro or VIP subscription
        Version 2.0 doesn't need it. Cancel it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
