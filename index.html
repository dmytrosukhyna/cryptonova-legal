How the system works
Four stages run without a break. Each one drops the weak part, so less comes out than went in.

    
    
      
  1. 01
    Collection
    The system collects market data: coin prices and volumes, futures markets, the Fear & Greed index and news.
    More
    Sources: the Binance, OKX and BingX exchanges, Hyperliquid, CoinGecko and news sites. It watches the market's 150 largest coins.
    2. 
      
  2. 02
    Analysis
    Every few minutes the system reads the market: trend, support levels, volume, bitcoin's mood and coin quality.
    More
    Coins in the spot portfolio go through a quality check: the project's economics, team activity, liquidity and price momentum. NOVA AI explains the conclusions in plain words.
    3. 
      
  3. 03
    Verification
    A new rule is first tested on history, on every coin including delisted ones, fees included. Then it trades on demo desks at real prices.
    More
    14 demo desks are running in parallel right now. Most ideas fail and are never used. Before every entry the system checks its risk limits.
    4. 
      
  4. 04
    Selection
    What passes becomes the system's rules. By them an autopilot runs the public portfolio on OKX, and the app shows the trades and their reasons.
    More
    The account is split like this: spot portfolio 65%, extra core BTC, ETH and SOL 10%, futures 15% (long trades only, at most 1% of the futures budget at risk per trade, leverage 2x, capped at 3x), active spot «BTC momentum» 10%, which waits in BTC between trades.

    Questions
    
    
    Do I have to pay?
    No. The app is free: the public portfolio, demo, lessons, piggy bank and NOVA AI (5 questions a day), with no subscriptions or purchases. If you copy CRYPTO NOVA on OKX, the exchange keeps a share of your profit for the lead trader; the percentage is shown in the profile before you start.
    Does the app trade for me?
    No. The app doesn't trade in your account, doesn't see it and doesn't hold money. Only OKX itself can repeat trades in your account, if you turn on copy trading. You can stop it in OKX at any time.
    Is the public portfolio real?
    Yes. It's CRYPTO NOVA's real account on OKX: history since 22 October 2026, starting capital $10,024.40. The system's autopilot places the trades. Deposits and withdrawals are not part of the result. Losing days and trades are shown just like winning ones.
    Where is my money?
    In demo there's no money: the amounts are virtual. If you trade or copy for real, the money sits in your own exchange account. CRYPTO NOVA doesn't hold it and can't withdraw it. We never ask for passwords, SMS codes, seed phrases or API keys.
    Can I copy from my country?
    Copy trading is an OKX product. The availability of copy trading and of some instruments depends on your country and region — OKX shows the terms. CRYPTO NOVA's trades are visible in the app and in the OKX profile, and you can repeat them yourself. Where OKX doesn't serve customers, the app offers BingX if it works in your country; copying there comes later.
    Is this financial advice?
    No. CRYPTO NOVA shows how the system works, explains the market and teaches. You make the decisions. Trading crypto is high risk: you can lose all the money you put in.
    What if the system is wrong?
    It makes mistakes, like any system, and they aren't hidden: losing trades are shown next to winning ones. Futures trades have a stop, and each trade uses only part of the account. There are no guarantees.
    Is there an Android version?
    Not yet. The Android version is in closed testing on Google Play and can't be installed. Right now CRYPTO NOVA is on iPhone and iPad in the App Store.
    NOVA AI didn't answer. What now?
    If the server or the AI model doesn't answer in time, the app shows a message and a «Retry» button, and the question isn't counted. Try again in a minute. If it still doesn't work, write to support.
    Who is behind CRYPTO NOVA?
    The founder and developer of the app is Dmytro Sukhyna. Write to cryptonova.app@gmail.com or on Telegram @crypto_nova_support.
    I still have an old Pro or VIP subscription
    Version 2.0 doesn't need it. To cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
