How to delete your CRYPTO NOVA account

The CRYPTO NOVA AI app (com.cryptonova, com.cryptonova.app) for iPhone, iPad and Android.

In the app, in 3 steps

  1. Open the app and tap the round letter icon at the top right. Account opens.
  2. Scroll down and tap “Delete account”.
  3. Confirm with “Delete”.

Deleted at once: your sign-in (Apple or Google), Piggy bank settings, lessons and XP, notification settings and push tokens, the NOVA question count and the link to your exchange UID.

Not affected: your exchange account. It is yours, and only you control it.

Without the app

Email cryptonova.app@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete everything within 30 days and confirm by email. Firebase keeps analytics and crash reports for up to 14 months in pseudonymised form.
