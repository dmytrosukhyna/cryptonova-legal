Privacy policy

Updated 22.10.2026. Applies to the CRYPTO NOVA AI app for iPhone, iPad and Android (bundle com.cryptonova, package com.cryptonova.app) and to the websites cryptonova.pro and cryptonova.vip.

In short. We keep only what the app needs: your sign-in, Piggy bank settings, completed lessons and notification settings. CRYPTO NOVA holds no money, cards, passwords or exchange keys. Files for “Portfolio review” are processed in memory and not stored. You can delete your account in the app with one button: Account → “Delete account”. The website sets no cookies and runs no trackers.

The app and the websites are built and run by Dmytro Sukhyna (“we”), founder and developer, who is the data controller. Contact: cryptonova.app@gmail.com.

2. NOVA AI assistant

The text of your question, together with general CRYPTO NOVA demo figures, is sent to an AI model provider (Groq or the Google Gemini API) to produce the answer. Your name and email are not sent. We do not store question or answer texts, only the number of questions per day to apply the limit. Please don’t include personal data in questions.

By voice. NOVA’s answers may be read aloud by the phone’s own speech synthesizer. The app does not use the microphone or speech recognition.

3. Portfolio review

In NOVA AI you can send a portfolio export or statement — a CSV, XLSX or PDF file — or a screenshot, up to 10 MB, and get a review. The file is processed in our server’s memory and is not saved: we neither store nor log the file or its contents. A screenshot or the text of a PDF may be sent to an AI model provider (Groq, or the Google Gemini API if enabled) only for recognition, without your name or email. Do not upload files containing passwords, API keys, seed phrases or identity documents.

4. Tracking and advertising

The app includes the Meta (Facebook) SDK. iOS asks whether you allow tracking. If you allow it, the advertising identifier and app events (install, launch, sign-in) are shared with Meta Platforms to measure ads. If you decline, no identifier is shared and Meta receives only aggregated data via Apple SKAdNetwork. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. There are no third-party ads in the app. We do not sell personal information.

5. The website

cryptonova.pro sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising pixels or trackers. Your language choice is saved in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to us. The live figures on the page are public demo data loaded from cryptonova.vip; they contain no personal data. Fonts are served from cryptonova.pro itself. Some public data pages on cryptonova.vip, such as the forecast table, load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when you open them.

Both websites are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes your IP address and technical request data to deliver the pages and protect the service, under the GitHub privacy statement. We do not receive these logs.

7. Legal basis

  • Providing the app (sign-in, Piggy bank, lessons, notifications, NOVA, Portfolio review): performance of our agreement with you.
  • Analytics and crash reports: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it.
  • Advertising identifier: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in iOS settings.
  • Answering your emails and legal obligations: legitimate interest and legal duty.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept while your account exists.
  • Delete your account in the app: Account → “Delete account”. Your sign-in, Piggy bank, lessons, settings, push tokens, question count and the link to your exchange UID, if you entered one in an earlier version of the app, are deleted from our server at once. Details: how to delete an account.
  • No access to the app? Email cryptonova.app@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. We delete within 30 days.
  • Firebase keeps analytics and crash data for up to 14 months under its own policy.
  • We keep support emails and Telegram chats for up to 12 months after the last message, then delete them.

9. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion at cryptonova.app@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. EU/EEA and UK residents have rights under the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawing consent) and may complain to the data protection authority of their country. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA; sharing the advertising identifier with Meta may count as “sharing”, and you can opt out by declining tracking.

10. Security

Traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Every request is checked with a Firebase token. Only the founder has server access. No system is perfectly secure, so please enable two-factor authentication on your exchange.

11. Children

The app is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect children’s data. If a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

Our server is in the European Union. The services in section 6 may process data in the United States and other countries under their own safeguards, such as the EU standard contractual clauses.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and announce material changes in the app.
