Frequent questions

Do I have to pay? No. The app is free: demo accounts, lessons, the Piggy bank and the NOVA AI assistant (5 questions a day). If you copy CRYPTO NOVA through OKX copy trading, OKX keeps a share of your profit for the lead trader; the percentage is shown in the profile before you start.

Where is my money? There is no money in the app: demo accounts use virtual money at real prices. If you open your own exchange account, your money always stays there. We don’t hold it and can’t withdraw it.

Does the app trade for me? No. The app doesn’t trade in your account and doesn’t see it. Only OKX itself can repeat CRYPTO NOVA’s trades in your account, if you turn on copy trading; you can stop it in OKX at any time.

How do I sign in? Account (the round button at the top right) → “Sign in with Apple” (iPhone, iPad) or Google (Android). Or right in the NOVA chat, where the sign-in button appears. Sign-in is needed for NOVA and to keep your Piggy bank and lessons on any phone; the demo amount is stored on the phone itself.

How do I connect a real account? In the app, open “Connect”: sign up on OKX through our link, verify your identity, deposit USDT and tap «Copy CRYPTO NOVA» — OKX will repeat the trades in your account for the amount you choose. No UID or API keys are needed, and you can stop copying in OKX at any time. The availability of copy trading and of some instruments depends on your country and region — OKX shows the terms. The trades are visible in the app, and you can repeat them yourself. We never ask for passwords, SMS codes or seed phrases.

How do I delete my account? In the app: Account → “Delete account” at the bottom → “Delete”. Details: how to delete an account.

How do I change the language? Account → “Language”: English, Русский, Українська, Ελληνικά, Español.
