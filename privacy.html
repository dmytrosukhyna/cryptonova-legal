2. How we use information

3. Tracking and advertising

The App includes the Meta (Facebook) SDK. On first launch iOS asks whether you allow the App to track you. If you allow, the device advertising identifier and app events (install, launch, registration, subscription) are shared with Meta Platforms to measure and optimize our ads. If you decline, no advertising identifier is shared and Meta receives only aggregated, non-identifying attribution data via Apple's SKAdNetwork. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. We do not show third-party ads inside the App.

5. Exchange API keys

API keys are optional and needed only for the auto-trading bot. They are transmitted over HTTPS and stored on our server to execute trades on your behalf. We recommend creating keys with trading permission only and without withdrawal permission. You can delete your keys at any time in Profile → Bot management, or by deleting your account.

6. Data retention

Account data is kept while your account exists. Crash and analytics data is retained by the respective provider for up to 14 months (Firebase) or as described in their policies. When you delete your account, your account record, portfolio, API keys and push token are deleted from our server within 30 days; backups are purged within 90 days.
